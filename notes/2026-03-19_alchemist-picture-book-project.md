# Project: The Alchemist — AI Picture Book

## Concept
A painted picture book / comic adaptation of *The Alchemist* by Paulo Coelho, generated with AI. Painting style, warm desert palette, consistent character design throughout.

## Style Direction
- **Influences**: Moebius, Shaun Tan (*The Arrival*), classic watercolor storybooks
- **Palette**: Gold, amber, warm earth tones, soft desert light
- **Treatment**: Loose expressive brushwork, painterly, cinematic composition
- **Consistency**: Same character design for Santiago, same color treatment across all scenes

## Base Prompt Template
> [scene description], painted storybook illustration, warm desert palette of gold and amber, loose expressive brushwork, soft light, cinematic composition

## Scene List
1. Santiago tending sheep in Andalusia
2. The recurring dream of the Pyramids
3. Meeting Melchizedek (the old king) in Tarifa
4. The crystal merchant's shop in Tangier
5. The desert caravan
6. Meeting Fatima at the oasis
7. The alchemist and the test of the Soul of the World
8. The Pyramids — and the twist ending
