# Cold Call Comeback

A co-worker was cold-calling customers trying to drum up business. One of them told him to "go fuck his hand."

He responded: "I've got that penciled in for 3. I should be done by 4 if you'd like to come in for an appointment then?"

The guy laughed his ass off — and ended up coming in for the appointment.
